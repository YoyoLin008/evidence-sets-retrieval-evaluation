The original study's cohorts, source mappings, five methods, six budgets and scoring definitions are frozen in `planning/analysis_plan.md`, `configs/main_v1.json`, `logs/main_freeze.json` and `logs/frozen_main_v1/`. This was a local prospective freeze, not public preregistration. The primary output contains 5,420 rankings and 32,520 scored points.

The joint answer-agreement/minimal-reference sensitivity was a post-main amendment (`planning/amendment_01.md`, `analysis/amendment_01_freeze.json`). Disjoint topology, bounded implementation audit and the original Qwen timing gate are previous post-main additions in `revisions/v2/`. The gate originally omitted effectiveness scoring; that historical decision is preserved and superseded by the explicitly authorized complete v1.1 experiment.

`revisions/v3/plan.md` and stream-specific protocols record what was known before the new analyses. The within-group pruning contrasts, uniform budgets, real conversion/scoring replay, Qwen full-cohort run and new deterministic example are revision-added exploratory evidence. They are not retrospective original preregistration. All six methods together have 6,504 rankings and 39,024 points at the original budgets; continuous integer-budget analysis is additional.

The real converter replay uses an explicit normalized-text quotient and candidate restriction, documents duplicate/abstract-position mappings, and verifies union membership separately. The native exported task is broader. Its ordinary relevance metrics are appropriate; no mismatch between its stated objective and implementation was established. No complete downstream product or generator was run.

Exact answer agreement is textual, not semantic. The complement is not a label of semantic conflict. The 8.2-point pruned restricted-cohort result is neither a mathematical bound on the original full-cohort gap nor a causal decomposition. Removal of strict supersets preserves C and d_C, but need not preserve best-reference F1.

Literature review credits prior complete-set success, Minimal Sufficient Rank and group-versus-union comparisons. The contribution is bounded paired measurement, structural decomposition, real representation tracing and an explicit uniform-budget interpretation. Annotation completion is not independently verified semantic sufficiency or measured human effort. Additional automated checks are not independent human review.
