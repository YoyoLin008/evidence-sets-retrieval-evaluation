# IRRJ submission checklist — v1.1.0

Author instructions consulted: https://irrj.org/about/submissions (3 October 2026). Normal Paper is the intended article type, with approximately twenty pages as the target rather than artificial font reduction.

- Use `paper/manuscript_official_style.pdf` as the main manuscript.
- Use `paper/supplement_official_style.pdf` as the supplementary material.
- Use `paper/cover_letter.pdf` for the required cover letter.
- Keep `paper/manuscript_reading_copy.pdf` for convenient reading only; it is not the official-style upload.
- Editable sources and figures accompany the reproducibility archive; official style is unchanged.
- Title, Yunya Lin, UIUC affiliation and corresponding-author email are consistent.
- Abstract, conclusions, cover letter and tables use the same verified machine-readable results.
- Original, prior post-main and revision-added exploratory results are distinguished.
- Human authorship, AI disclosure, funding, originality, concurrent-review and conflict declarations preserve the confirmed account; no additional personal assertions were invented.
- Third-party licenses are excluded from MIT/CC BY grants; data/weights have pinned acquisition instructions.
- Frozen files cite the verified concept DOI plus exact v1.1.0 tag. The new version DOI is added only after it exists, in mutable landing documentation.
- The release validation report records test, rendering, visual and remote-download status. It must show actual completion before using the package.

Not performed: logging into or submitting to IRRJ, accepting journal terms, selecting submission-system declarations, or communicating with editors. The author must perform those final submission actions and review the actual form requirements at submission time.
