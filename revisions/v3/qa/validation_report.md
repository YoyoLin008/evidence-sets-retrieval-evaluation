## Skeptical claim audit and readiness

The contribution is a controlled, annotation-defined measurement audit. Complete-set success, sufficient rank and group-versus-union evaluation have prior methodological precedents. The 8.2-point result is a restricted cohort/target description, not a bound or causal decomposition. A flat relevance export cannot generally recover C, but conventional relevance metrics remain valid for their stated objectives. The real replay changes native candidate scope and normalizes equivalent text; it does not establish widespread evaluator defects. Uniform budget thresholds are not human reading time or inference savings. Full Qwen results retain zero median extra depth and no new primary F1 reversal in the fixed comparisons. Every new result is exploratory.

The files are ready for the authorized release once asset and remote checks pass, and for the author's final submission review. Remaining scientific limitations are semantic validity of annotations, strict cohort selection, one additional encoder, possible model pretraining overlap, exploratory pointwise intervals, and a bounded implementation sample. Independent human semantic annotation, external/cross-platform replication, a complete downstream product run and formal IRRJ submission were not performed and are not claimed. No acceptance probability is assigned.
