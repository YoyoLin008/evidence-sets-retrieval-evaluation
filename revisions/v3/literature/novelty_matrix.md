## Newly located work

RARE (Cho and Lee, ACL 2026) uses atomic information equivalence to track alternatives and evaluates redundancy-aware coverage and complete recall (§3, §5.1). This is another precedent for richer evidence targets, not the fixed-family C/A audit here. Published source: https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.923/ .

The Missing Complement (Feng et al., September 2026 preprint) defines grouped requirements with thresholds and complete alternative branches for coding-agent states (§3). This further rules out broad claims to inventing set completion. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20050v1 .

RINSE (Chen et al., September 2026 preprint) evaluates sufficiency scorers with paired substitution, deletion and question-swap contrasts (§3–4). It changes semantic support, whereas this audit holds retrieved evidence fixed and changes the annotation target. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37469v1 .

Those three additions were checked in the original methods, not read in full. Their results were not independently reproduced. The bounded search found no reason to assert stronger novelty; it is not an exhaustive absence claim.
