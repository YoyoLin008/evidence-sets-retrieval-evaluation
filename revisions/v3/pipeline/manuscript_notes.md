# Evidence for manuscript integration

Status: revision-added exploratory conversion and scoring replay. The selection plan preceded new outcomes and acknowledged prior v1.0.0 familiarity. The two bounded candidates were the already audited sscc-rag-paper implementation and MTEB/QASPER distribution. The predeclared rule preferred an executable converter/scorer over a distribution without its construction code. MTEB was not selected; its historical 576/1,335 statistic is not reused as a main-cohort denominator.

The selected implementation was qarkapp/sscc-rag-paper, commit da7e8fb13f297969d344bad90295b15c2917a47d. Its native loader accepts train/dev; we first ran the unchanged dev path (888 exported queries, 13,547 corpus paragraphs). A data-supply wrapper produced exactly the same structures on dev. We then supplied the original frozen test JSON through that interface without changing converter logic. The converter and actual source JSONL loader produced byte-equivalent serialized structures after export/reload. The native task pools abstracts and body paragraphs across papers; the controlled score replay explicitly restricted candidate IDs to the original paper-body universe and reused saved rankings. It therefore reproduces conversion and scoring, not the full pooled retriever, RAPTOR index, generator, or original published downstream results.

The real test input contained 1,451 questions from 416 papers. The converter exported 1,309 queries and 20,233 paragraph IDs. Of all raw questions, 1,012 had exactly mapped complete source membership, 297 were exported with unmapped evidence strings, and 142 were not exported. These all-raw figures differ from the original eligible cohort. All 875 original eligible questions appeared in the export; 866 questions (364 paper clusters) had complete normalized-text candidate alignment and exact source-union/export-membership equality. Of these, 464 had multiple distinct evidence sets and 402 had one distinct set. Nine questions (14 evidence units) failed membership equality because the converter's strip-only matching missed evidence that matched after the original adapter's whitespace normalization. Their IDs and evidence hashes remain visible. Within the 866 matched questions, 90 have duplicate/normalized-equivalent output texts (four involve gold); eight map a body unit to an identical abstract text ID (one involves gold) because the first matching output ID is canonical. The controlled universe is a quotient by the original normalized body-text units, not a claim of one-to-one raw paragraph positions. All 875 have an explicit candidate-scope change relative to the full upstream task; candidate scope is reported separately from membership equality. No native grouped or paired grouped/aggregated representation is exposed on this converter path.

At k=5 on the 866 aligned questions, BM25 achieved H=65.0%, C=51.4%, A=33.5%, and C−A=17.9 percentage points (155/866; paper-cluster 95% percentile interval 15.4–20.5 points). The actual scorer's Recall@5 was 46.4%, Success@5 was 65.0%, and nDCG@10 was 42.3%. These are different quantities: actual Recall@5 equals union overlap recall and actual Success@5 equals H. The scorer does not claim to calculate C or A. All six original default measures, all five methods and per-query outputs are retained. Direct formulas agreed with the source ir_measures/pytrec_eval scorer to at most 2.22e−16; aggregation also matched per-query means.

The MiniLM-minus-BM25 advantage was 3.70 points for C (95% cluster interval −0.34 to 7.68) and 0.23 points for A (−3.14 to 3.54). Best-reference and union-F1 differences remained positive (2.06 and 1.70 points respectively). No primary F1 rank reversal is created by this case. The conventional relevance objectives are explicitly documented in the upstream code; the case establishes information loss for reconstructing C, not a proven mismatch between the project's stated relevance objective and its implementation. It supports a bounded practical warning and demonstrates a change in the magnitude of a completion-rate comparison.

Authoritative values and intervals are in score_summary.csv and paired_contrasts.csv; display rounding above is descriptive. report.json records inputs, code hashes, counts, environment, scope and scorer checks. main_cohort_alignment.json records all 875 equality tests and exclusions, including source IDs and text-hash evidence. No new semantic annotations or independent human audit are claimed.
